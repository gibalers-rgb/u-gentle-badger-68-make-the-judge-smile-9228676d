## The entry

> My neighbour's cat, who has ignored me for two years, chose this morning to sit on my shoes and refuse to move — I missed my bus and grinned all the way to work on foot.

(169 characters.)

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` and refuses to write anything that would fail the gate: empty, whitespace-only, over 200 characters, or with leading/trailing whitespace.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Why this line

The judge weighs, most important first: on subject, warmth, surprise, one concrete detail, fresh wording. The line is built for that order:

- **On subject:** a small good thing that happened this morning.
- **Warmth:** an aloof cat finally choosing a person — a tiny kindness that lands as genuinely felt.
- **Surprise:** two years of being ignored, then *today* it claims my shoes.
- **Concreteness:** the cat, the shoes, the missed bus, the walk to work.
- **Fresh wording:** no greeting-card phrasing; the grin costs something (a missed bus) and is worth it.
